**Full description** (max 4000):

Remember the snake game on your old phone? It is back: faster, prettier and full of surprises!

Swipe to turn, eat apples to grow and do not crash. Easy to learn, hard to put down.

GAME MODES
- Classic: level up every 8 apples. Each level brings new obstacles and more speed.
- VS CPU: duel a computer controlled snake. Reach 15 apples first or make your rival crash!

POWER-UPS
- Slow: slows the game down for a few seconds.
- Ghost: pass through blocks, bodies and walls.
- Double: double points for 10 seconds.
- Scissors: cuts 4 pieces off your tail.
- Shield: saves you from one crash.
- Golden apple: +50 points, but it vanishes fast!

HIGHLIGHTS
- Retro look with smooth movement and animations.
- Swipe or on-screen button controls.
- Three difficulty levels: Easy, Normal and Hard.
- High scores saved on your device.
- Plays offline, no ads and no data collection.
- Lightweight: runs well even on simple phones.
